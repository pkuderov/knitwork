\section{Resource Accounting}\label{app:resources}

Resource accounting covers the 48 runs with confirmed NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3 logs among the 65 larger-scale main and reduced-token runs. The quality tables include all runs; resource tables exclude the 17 runs on other GPU types. Hardware-restricted counts therefore differ from quality-table counts.

For processed-token counts $s_i$ and logged interval rates $f_i$, estimated loop time is $T_{\mathrm{loop}}=\sum_i(s_i-s_{i-1})/f_i$, with $s_0=0$. Effective rate is processed tokens divided by this time. The estimate includes compilation, training, evaluation, inspection, logging, and workload contention; it measures elapsed loop time rather than exclusive GPU-hours.

Update counts are $\lfloor s_{\mathrm{last}}/(n_{\mathrm{envs}}\,\ell_{\mathrm{TBPTT}})\rfloor$, counting reached update boundaries, including any skipped nonfinite-gradient updates. Tables report means and sample standard deviations across runs; single-run rows have no variability estimate. Shared hardware labels do not control concurrent workloads. The 1M and pilot experiments also report H100 80GB HBM3.
